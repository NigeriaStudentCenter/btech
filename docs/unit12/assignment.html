5. Assignment builder

Plan each task, keep your evidence organised, and get feedback on your own drafts from the AI assignment coach. Your work saves on this device.

This is your workspace, not a shortcut. The coach explains, asks questions and tells you what is missing; it will not write your code, designs or reviews. Every coach question is saved in your AI-use log: download it and hand it in with your work, as your teacher asks.

My AI-use log

A record of every question you asked the assignment coach, with the date and task. Hand this in if your teacher asks how you used AI.

    The assignment coach uses an AI model provided by the course. It can make mistakes; check technical details against your lessons and test your own code. Your teacher assesses your work.
