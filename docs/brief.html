1. Your assignment and practice brief

Your computing challenge

A community learning centre needs computers for learning, bookings and sharing information. You will learn how computers work, practise with small examples, and explain choices using evidence.

This is your course practice brief. Unit 2 is assessed by an external examination. These activities help you prepare; they are not an official Pearson assignment or a substitute for that exam.

What to do

  1. Read the learning page for your section. Look at the diagram and try the worked example.
  2. Open the matching workbook section. Complete the activity and quick check in your own words. Show your working for calculations.
  3. Ask the Azure tutor for an explanation or one hint if you are stuck. Use your own answer.
  4. Write a short note about what you understand and what you need to practise.
  5. Download a backup, then use the revision practice for that topic.

What to hand in

Follow your teacher’s Teams assignment instructions. You can download your workbook backup, or print the requested section to PDF. If an activity needs a diagram, draw it on paper or in your school’s drawing tool and submit that separately as your teacher directs. The text workbook does not upload drawing files.

Your teacher will set lesson dates, deadlines and which sections to submit. Do not put your name or contact details into this app. Your school’s Teams handles your submission.

How to check your work

  • Use the correct computing words and explain what they mean.
  • Give reasons linked to the situation, not just a list of facts.
  • Label diagrams clearly and show calculation steps.
  • Check your answer and improve it after feedback.
