5. Past Exam Papers

Five real Unit 2 exam papers from Pearson. Each one is 80 marks and lasts 1 hour 45 minutes, just like your exam.

  1. Choose a paper and open the question paper (it opens on Pearson’s website).
  2. Answer each part in the boxes below. Your answers save on this device as you type.
  3. Stuck? Use the AI tutor on that part: Help me start, Check my answer or, for 6–12 mark questions, Plan my long answer. It will not write the answer for you.
  4. When you have finished, open the mark scheme and the examiner’s report, and record the mark you think you earned.

Question papers, mark schemes and examiner’s reports are © Pearson Education Ltd and are opened from Pearson’s own website. The topic notes on this page are our own summaries. Do not type your name or personal details.
